How to read this. Media relations = the named press contact for that geography (from official media-contact pages and press-release contact blocks, usually with a published email). Comms head = the regional Head/Director/VP of Corporate Communications / Corporate Affairs / Marketing & Communications — the people who decide who represents the firm externally. Exec-visibility = brand-podcast hosts/producers, content & thought-leadership chiefs — the doors for getting executives (or joint content) on air. Agency rows are flagged: pitch them as the route in, not the decision-maker.

Honest caveats. (1) 65 of the 210 rows were re-verified against live sources on compile day (10 page fetches + 4 news checks, zero failures); the rest are agent-sourced from the same day with sample checks passed. (2) Comms people churn fast — five 2025-26 moves were caught in this sweep alone (Accenture, TCS, Capgemini, LTM, Wipro→Cognizant); re-verify any row before outreach and refresh quarterly. (3) LinkedIn URLs were only captured when they appeared verbatim in search results — never constructed; blanks are honest blanks. (4) Emails/phones are published for press use. (5) “Gap” cells are findings, not laziness — MBB regional leads and Japan desks are structurally unpublished; each gap carries a where-to-look recipe in the drill-down. (6) LTIMindtree rebranded to LTM effective Feb 11, 2026 (ltm.com).
