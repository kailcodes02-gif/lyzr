## Executive Summary & Systemic Architecture

### Ideal ToFu to BoFu Funnel Engine (Powered by RB2B & Behavioral Intelligence)
* **Owners**: Primary: Kailash, Nirupam | Secondary: Vaibhav, Rishabh, Mothilal
* **Funnel Flow Mechanics & Architecture**:
  1. **Step 1: Traffic Ingestion (ToFu)**
     - 🎯 **Objective**: Drive high-volume, targeted top-of-funnel traffic to landing pages & key assets.
     - 📡 **Sources**: LinkedIn Brand Ads, Meta Ads (Traffic), Google Search/YT Ads, SEO/GEO.
     - ↳ Bring traffic from Brand Ads (LinkedIn), Traffic Ads (Meta & Google), and SEO/GEO.
     - ↳ Direct top-of-funnel visitors to primary solution pages, comparison pages, and hero assets.
  2. **Step 2: RB2B Cohort Segmentation**
     - 🎯 **Objective**: Identify visitor intent levels & isolate top-performing hero conversion pathways.
     - 📡 **Sources**: RB2B Identity Resolution & Behavioral Tracking Engine.
     - ↳ Divide traffic into P90, P75, P50 (first-time ever traffic) to find the hero flow.
     - ↳ Map visitor engagement depth and track high-intent user pathways across landing pages.
  3. **Step 3: LinkedIn Retargeting (MoFu)**
     - 🎯 **Objective**: Re-engage non-converting visitors with stage-specific educational resources.
     - 📡 **Sources**: LinkedIn Retargeting Ads (Rishabh).
     - ↳ Retarget traffic cohorts for journey-specific resources on LinkedIn.
     - ↳ Drive visitors directly to targeted playbooks and gated architecture blueprints.
  4. **Step 4: Behavioral Email Nurture**
     - 🎯 **Objective**: Nurture warm retargeted prospects based on micro-interactions.
     - 📡 **Sources**: Instantly & Hubspot Email Sequences (Kailash, Nirupam).
     - ↳ Target P50 of retargeted cohorts (non-first time).
     - ↳ Personalized sequence for P50 of retargeted cohorts (non-first time) based on hero landing page events (non "Book a Demo" & non-playbook downloads).
  5. **Step 5: LinkedIn Social Outreach**
     - 🎯 **Objective**: Build direct 1-1 executive relationships on LinkedIn with warm prospects.
     - 📡 **Sources**: Executive Profiles (Anju, Ani, Praveen, Pooja, Kaushik, Bharath, Kailash).
     - ↳ Target P50 of retargeted pool (non-first time).
     - ↳ Send personalized LinkedIn connection request + contextual custom note.
  6. **Step 6: High-Touch Sales Handoff (BoFu)**
     - 🎯 **Objective**: Maximize conversion of high-intent prospects through direct cold calling.
     - 📡 **Sources**: Jessica’s SDR Cold Calling Team & Partnership Team.
     - ↳ Pass P90 of retargeted & playbook leads (that did not convert to book a demo).
     - ↳ Equip cold calling team with complete user behavior telemetry & activity logs.
