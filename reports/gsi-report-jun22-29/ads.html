What we tested, what worked, and what it cost.

Across an 11-day flight on LinkedIn, we executed a structured testing roadmap spanning brand awareness and lead generation for Lyzr's GSI & SI motion. Targeting, creative, and budget allocation were iterated in sequence to drive down delivery costs and isolate the highest-efficiency audience–creative combinations. This report documents every test, the full budget reconciliation, and the performance each layer returned.

05

Audience strategy & budget allocation

Two levers drove the efficiency gains: how budget was paced by objective, and a deliberate migration from uploaded custom lists to seniority-based native targeting once list economics proved unworkable at scale.

$ Budget pacing by objective

  • Brand awareness — $20–40 / day per ad set. Daily budgets were set against audience size, then re-weighted toward the best-performing campaigns as delivery signal matured.
  • Conversation ads — $50–70 / day per ad set. Sponsored Messaging send delivery is budget-gated: the LinkedIn delivery system scales send volume with spend, so a higher daily floor is required to grow sends and opens.
  • Reallocation discipline. Underperforming custom-list and playbook sets were paused and their budget redeployed to the audiences clearing the auction efficiently.

⇄ Custom → native migration

  • Custom lists ran hot. Match-rate-adjusted list sizes were small (see below), thinning the auction and inflating CPMs to $190–450.
  • Conversation ads → native, grouped in pairs at the MD-1 / MD-2 seniority tier — Group 1: Accenture (AU + Nordic) + Fujitsu; Group 2: KPMG + TCS — to build the audience scale send delivery requires.
  • Brand awareness → native by seniority, targeting MD-1 / MD-2-equivalent job titles mapped to the same GSI & SI accounts.

10

What we learned

Consolidated takeaways across audiences, creative, budget, and conversion.

  • Audience pool size is the primary CPM lever. Custom lists below ~800 match-adjusted members pushed CPMs to $190–$450. The only efficient custom list (Kaushik, 7,695 members) cleared at $22 CPM. Pooling into combined sets compressed cost further — the combined-native set delivered at $18 CPM, roughly 5× cheaper than small lists.
  • Native targeting outperformed custom lists on every efficiency metric. Attribute-based native sets produced the strongest CTR and the only qualified lead. Combined-native optimises cost and volume; native-only optimises engagement.
  • NTT Data and Accenture creatives anchored performance. NTT Data-1 drove the highest click volume (32 clicks). The Accenture creative led on click rate (1.63% pooled; 3.35% standalone) — named-account social proof consistently outperformed generic category messaging. Playbook units contributed 31 combined awareness clicks.
  • Geography signal is clear. India dominated impression volume, but Australia delivered 2.08–3.41% CTR on Playbook-1 and the Accenture creative. UK and Australia showed the strongest open rates in messaging (47–58%).
  • Conversation ads confirmed inbox access to the right seniority. Managing Director and Associate Director received the majority of sends. Open rates of 38% (peaking at 57%) confirm delivery is working — but 0% click-to-open means the CTA is the gap, not targeting.
  • Ani outperformed Jessica across every conversion metric. Ani delivered more sends, more opens, and the program's only qualified lead (Ani · Accenture · Native). Jessica's India-native sets were the weakest performers (17–24% open rate).
  • Budget dispersal across too many campaigns diluted impact. Running awareness, playbook lead-gen, conversation ads, and website-visit messaging in parallel prevented any single objective from reaching the spend threshold needed to convert.
11

Next month — campaign optimisation plan

Based on what the data showed, here is how we recommend restructuring the campaign for July to drive qualified leads.

  • Concentrate ~80% of budget on conversation ads — lead-gen form only. The single qualified lead came from Ani · Accenture · Native via a lead-gen form. Pause website-visit messaging (booking demo page returned 0 clicks) and redirect that budget entirely to the lead-gen form objective.
  • Increase daily budget to $80–$120 per ad set. Sponsored Messaging delivery is spend-gated. Current budgets were too low to generate consistent send volume. A $80–$120 daily floor is required to drive meaningful sends and build pipeline, especially in markets outside India where the single lead originated.
  • Prioritise Ani on native targeting and run at least 3 message variations. Ani · Accenture · Native is the only converting segment — optimise this before scaling Jessica. Open rates are strong (38%) but click-to-open is 0%, so the message copy and offer need testing across the same audience simultaneously.
  • Allocate ~20% of budget to brand awareness on combined-native targeting. Run one combined-native ad set at MD-1 / MD-2 seniority level targeting GSI & SI job functions — this format delivered $18 CPM, the most efficient in the program. Retire all custom lists below 1,000 match-adjusted members.
  • Drive awareness traffic to playbooks, case studies, and the GSI & SI landing page. Playbook-1, Playbook-2, and the NTT Data and Accenture case study pages are the highest-intent destinations. Routing awareness clicks to these pages — rather than the homepage — increases the chance of a tracked conversion from an awareness impression.
  • Lead with NTT Data and Accenture creatives; retire zero-click units. These two creative formats drove the strongest CTR. Retire "Why GSIs Are Building on Lyzr?", "Startup Onboarding, Automated", and other zero-click units. New creatives should follow the same named-account, outcome-led format.
