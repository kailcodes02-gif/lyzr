Three Learnings This Week
1 · Post amplification is the standout — by a wide margin
  • Anju's amplified post pulled a 2.04% CTR and 125 clicks — more clicks than every other set combined, at the lowest CPM ($7.09). Amplifying a real person's content dramatically out-performs static brand creative (next-best CTR was 0.62%)
  • Takeaway: make founder/leader post-amplification a permanent line in the GSI/SI paid mix — it is the most efficient reach-and-click unit we have run
2 · Low-cost geos are winning the awareness efficiency race
  • India/UAE beat US/UK/EU on both CPM ($7.13 vs $11.71) and CTR (0.62% vs 0.50%), delivering ~34% more reach for similar budget
  • Keep both geos live for coverage, but the low-cost split is the efficient scale lever for top-of-funnel reach; reserve the high-cost geo for the named priority accounts
3 · Two new plays launched — Agentic Roadmap + GSI Survey now have paid support
  • The Agentic Roadmap awareness set and the GSI Survey set both went live this flight, tying paid distribution to the roadmap (now live) and the survey (phantom live). Early CPMs are higher ($14.89 / $8.10) as they find their audience — expect these to settle with optimization
  • Ani's priority-account conversation ads opened at 101 sends / 29 opens (28.7%) — a healthy early open rate for a cold conversation flow
