💡 TCS India — the benchmark
  • Best-in-class: 39.61% open, 5.06% click, 11 replies across 356 sends. Step 2 holds at 32.98% open — the sequence has legs, not just a strong first touch
  • Action: clone this campaign's subject lines, sender identity and send-time as the template for KPMG/Accenture rebuilds. Push replies to sales follow-up fast while warm
🔴 Underperformers — fixes & experiments
  • Accenture Australia (0% open, 130 sent): a 0% open almost always = deliverability/spam placement or broken tracking, not copy. Check domain warmup, SPF/DKIM/DMARC and inbox placement before sending more. Re-verify the list and run a 10–20 send seed test
  • Accenture Nordic (1.04% open, 96 sent): same deliverability signal. Oddly 3 clicks on 1 open suggests tracking misfire — audit the open-pixel/click tracking. Warm the sending mailbox and re-test with a plain-text first touch
  • KPMG India (9.38% open, 448 sent): large list, weak open — subject line + sender reputation. Experiment: A/B 3 new subject lines, switch to a person-name sender, and split the 448 into smaller daily batches to protect domain reputation. Step 2 opened 100% on 5 sends, so engaged sub-segment exists — isolate and nurture it
  • Fujitsu June (31.67% open, 0% click): open is fine, zero clicks = the ask/CTA or link isn't compelling. Experiment: add a single clear CTA with a calendar link, tighten body to 3–4 lines, and test a value-led PS line
  • 8 Tracks — Gulf GSI (43% open, 0 click, 30 sent): tiny volume, good open. Add a soft CTA and scale the list before judging
