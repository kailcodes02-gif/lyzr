The window in short

Key takeaways

What's working

  • Native targeting is the efficient path. The 2 active native sets (Fujitsu, KPMG) deliver volume at far lower cost — KPMG Native hit $41 CPM and $5.47 CPC, the account's best paid efficiency.
  • NTT Data creative is performing well across both active ad sets, delivering a ~5% CTR overall — significantly above the account average of 0.81%, making it the strongest performing unit in the native targeting sets.
  • Creative refresh underway. Native sets launched on the CB Insights creative; the two new final assets were activated later, so all active sets now run 3 creative variations.
  • Kaushik List was the lone scale exception. Its custom audience exceeded 5,000 members, so the pool was large enough to deliver 1,536 reach / 2,565 impressions at just $22 CPM — the cheapest in the account. The narrower lists never reached that scale, which kept their CPMs high.

Why we paused the lists

  • Narrow custom lists pushed CPM 2–3× above benchmark — most list sets ran $190–$275 CPM vs. the $92 average, as tight targeting shrank the deliverable pool.
  • Low engagement compounded it — 46 clicks & 50 engagements across 5,705 impressions, with 0 leads or conversions.
  • Worst outliers: Fujitsu List ($273 CPM, $39.94 CPC) and Bharat List ($239 CPM, $25.91 CPC).

Where we go from here

Action steps — GSI & SI

Budget direction

Shift the main focus to conversation ads, with roughly 20% of budget held back for awareness campaigns. Spreading spend across too many top-of-funnel campaigns is diluting impact at the current spend level.

  1. 1
    Launch conversation ads today. Activate the conversation ads with the revised ad copies.
  2. 2
    Consolidate awareness targeting. Combine audiences into fewer awareness campaigns instead of diversifying spend across many top-of-funnel sets, so each can deliver tangible impact.
  3. 3
    Build a retargeting layer. Over the month, retarget post-engagers and website visitors with multiple impressions and drive them to download a resource such as a playbook.
  4. 4
    Concentrate on a few target companies. Given limited spend, focus on cracking the funnel for a small set of priority accounts first, then expand to the rest.
